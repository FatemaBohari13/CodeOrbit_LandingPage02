Style guide

The colors, type, spacing, and components used across this page.

Color palette

  • Midnight#101124Background
  • Violet#7957FFPrimary accent
  • Electric cyan#55E6FFTech accent, focus
  • Coral#FF8066Buttons, warmth
  • Soft white#F8F7FFMain text
  • Lavender#DCD2FFSecondary text, borders

Typography

  • Heading 1Poppins 700, 40 to 64 px
  • Heading 2Poppins 600, 32 to 48 px
  • Heading 3Poppins 600, 24 px
  • Body text for paragraphs and descriptions.Inter 400, 16 px
  • Small text for captions and notes.Inter 400, 14 px

Buttons

Primary Secondary

48 px tall, pill shape. Hover lifts the button, focus shows a cyan outline, and disabled drops to 50% opacity.

Spacing

Everything sits on an 8 px grid. Sections use 96 px of vertical space, cards use 24 px gaps.

Corner radius

Drag the slider or pick a preset to see the corners change on the sample card and button.
